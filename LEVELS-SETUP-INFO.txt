HIT LEVELS AND XP SETUP

Recommended first configuration:

/hit levels-setup

Log channel: #transcripts
Announcement channel: choose your public notifications or general channel
Message XP minimum: 15
Message XP maximum: 25
Message cooldown: 60 seconds
Voice XP per minute: 10
Voice minimum members: 2
Announce level ups: True
Stack reward roles: True

Then run:

/hit levels-diagnose
/hit levels-config

Public commands:

/level rank
/level leaderboard
/level rewards

;rank
;rank @user
;leaderboard
;levels rewards

Administration:

/hit levels-manage action:add-reward level:5 role:@Level 5
/hit levels-manage action:exclude-channel channel:#commands
/hit levels-manage action:exclude-role role:@Bots
/hit levels-manage action:add-xp user:@member amount:100

HIT does not award message XP to commands, bots, webhooks, link-only messages, excluded channels/categories, or excluded roles. Voice XP requires active, non-deafened members and is not awarded in the AFK channel.
