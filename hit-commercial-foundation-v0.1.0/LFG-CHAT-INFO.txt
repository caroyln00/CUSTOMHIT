# LFG CHAT

Use this channel to coordinate with players after creating or joining an LFG.

## HOW TO FIND A GROUP
1. Go to <#1528950639596408842>.
2. Press **Create LFG** and complete the guided form.
3. Open the LFG post and use the **Join** or **Leave** buttons.
4. Use this chat for quick coordination, then move to the correct voice channel or game lobby.

## CHAT RULES
- Keep messages related to finding, joining, and organizing groups.
- Respect the game, platform, region, rank, age, microphone, and skill requirements listed by the host.
- Do not spam, fake-join, troll groups, or repeatedly leave after taking a slot.
- No account buying, selling, trading, boosting, cheats, exploits, scams, paid carries, or suspicious links.
- Never share passwords, login codes, recovery codes, tokens, QR codes, or payment details.
- Do not harass players because of skill, voice, platform, gender, or region.
- Close your LFG when your group is full or finished.

Use `/lfg mine` to view your active posts. Staff may remove unsafe groups or members who misuse LFG.
