# HIT Feature-Parity Checklist

## Already inside HIT v97.21.43

- Verification, NV/YV role flow, CAPTCHA, account-age checks, lockouts
- Ticket panels, private tickets, claims, transcripts, closing and reopening
- Warnings, cases, timeout, kick, ban, purge, slowmode, lock and unlock
- Anti-spam, duplicate detection, mass-mention detection, anti-phishing
- Anti-raid, anti-nuke, trusted staff, quarantine, emergency lockdown
- Guided LFG posts, join and leave, capacity, expiration and archiving
- Multiple join-to-create lobbies and temporary voice-room controls
- Message and voice XP, ranks, leaderboards and reward roles
- Giveaways, rerolls, event scheduling and RSVP handling
- Economy, daily and work rewards, payments and leaderboard
- Counting and starboard
- Persistent SQLite storage and private logging
- Docker and Railway deployment for 24/7 operation

## Remaining commercial-bot parity work

The exact old-bot list must be checked before claiming one-to-one parity. Common remaining systems are:

- Welcome and leave messages with configurable images or embeds
- Reaction roles and button role menus
- Suggestions, polls and staff voting workflows
- Scheduled announcements, reminders and recurring messages
- Social feeds and game/news feeds
- Server statistics and automatic channel counters
- Custom commands, auto-responses and tag commands
- Modmail and anonymous staff replies
- Application forms and staff recruitment workflows
- Backup export, restore and scheduled off-site backup
- Music and queue controls
- Web dashboard, permissions and configuration history

Do not remove a third-party bot until its required features are tested in HIT with an alternate account.
